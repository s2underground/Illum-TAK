Illum for WinTAK

I ported the astronomy formulas from this repository's Java core. The references
are Jean Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms, 2nd edition (1998), chapters 12, 22, 25,
47 and 48, and USNO Circular 171, Janiczek and DeYoung (1987), for illuminance.
The USNO circular is public domain.

I reuse the repository's tzmap.bin, derived from timezone-boundary-builder
release 2026e by Evan Siroky, using OpenStreetMap data.
Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors.
The derived database is available under the Open Database License 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Source and rebuild instructions: tools/tzmap/ in this repository.

I include Noda Time 3.3.7 for offline IANA timezone rules on Windows.
Copyright the Noda Time Authors, Apache License 2.0.
https://github.com/nodatime/nodatime
License: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
The complete license is in licenses/NodaTime-LICENSE.txt in the source tree,
and NodaTime-LICENSE.txt in the WPK's plugin folder.
Noda Time includes IANA Time Zone Database data: https://www.iana.org/time-zones
